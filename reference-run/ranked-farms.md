## Scoring notes

**farm-10 (94)** — Highest total in the file. 7,900 acres, corn and soybeans,
formal 2027 input RFP going out in November, and a COO who has explicitly asked
whether a single program could cover all four units. Switching scores 5 on the
RFP alone. Technology posture held at 4 rather than 5: they think in
supply-chain terms, not agronomic ones, and there is no evidence of on-farm
trials.

**farm-01 (93)** — Contract expires after this harvest, incumbent has a named
and specific failure (standability, ~400 acres walked down in Aug 2025), and
Marcus asked for a price across 6,000 acres unprompted. That is the strongest
single switching signal in the file. Scale is 4 not 5 — 6,200 acres is below
the 7,000 threshold. Note that Dale is the owner on paper and Marcus makes the
input decisions; the rep's note resolves this clearly, so it is not an authority
flag.

**farm-03 (92)** — Full precision setup, subsurface drip on ~4,000 acres, runs
his own replicated check strips and brought three years of his own data
unprompted. Annual contract terms mean no lock-in. Relationship is 3: engaged
and warm but early, and there is an open technical question the rep could not
answer.

**farm-08 (92)** — Technically the most sophisticated buyer in the file: two
staff agronomists, a data analyst, 40 replicated test blocks. Switching is 4
rather than 5 — "fine, not special" is dissatisfaction, but mild, and no
contract event is pending. Relationship is 3 and should not be read as a
weakness to fix: Devon has said explicitly he does not want a sales
relationship.

**farm-05 (86), MEDIUM confidence** — Scores well and the opening is real:
Ingrid raised supplier consolidation herself. Portfolio fit is the constraint at
3 — soybeans are core, but spring wheat and canola sit outside the row-crop
trait portfolio this campaign is selling, so a material share of the 8,200 acres
is not addressable. MEDIUM confidence reflects that the addressable-acre split
was estimated from crop mix rather than known.

**farm-02 (82)** — Sixth, and the closest miss. Six-year customer, expanding,
and Renata has said she would move the corn across if the digital and agronomic
support is there. It ranks below the top five on scale (4,800 acres) rather than
on quality of opportunity. Worth noting for a campaign this one is not sized
for: this is the lowest-effort win in the file and the rubric is deliberately
built not to reward that.
