# Needs human review

Four of the ten farms in this file were **not scored**. Each one below could
have been given a number. In every case the number would have been misleading
rather than merely imprecise, so no number was produced.

These are not the bottom of the ranking. They are not in the ranking.

---

## farm-04 — Mueller Century Farm

- **Why this needs a person:** The CRM says 4,300 acres. The rep's own note says
  that stopped being true last autumn — a north parcel of roughly 2,000 acres
  was sold and a further block went to Wendell's daughter, who farms it
  separately under her own name. The rep's best guess is ~1,200 acres. Scale is
  25% of the rubric, so the difference between 4,300 and 1,200 moves this farm
  from mid-table to below the campaign's threshold entirely. Both numbers are in
  our own records and they cannot both be right.
- **The specific question a human has to answer:** What is Mueller Century
  Farm's current operated acreage, verified against Renville County records?
- **What we would do once it is answered:** Rescore in full. At ~1,200 acres it
  falls out of this campaign; at anything above ~4,000 it is a credible top-ten
  account with an unusually warm relationship attached.
- **Evidence:** *"the number in our system is wrong and I do not know what the
  right one is."*

## farm-06 — Blakemore Acres

- **Why this needs a person:** There is nothing to score. The territory was
  inherited from a rep who left in October 2025, no call notes were transferred,
  and nothing was found in the shared drive. The CRM row carries 3,700 acres, a
  crop mix, and an estimated annual spend of $0 — which is a placeholder, not a
  finding. Every rubric dimension except Scale would be scored on nothing at
  all. **This is the only farm on this list whose problem is an absence rather
  than a conflict**, and it is correspondingly the easiest one to score by
  accident.
- **The specific question a human has to answer:** Who owns this territory now,
  and does any record of contact with Blakemore Acres exist anywhere — the
  departed rep's files, the dealer, the co-op?
- **What we would do once it is answered:** Either score it properly on
  recovered notes, or treat it as a genuine cold prospect and qualify it from
  scratch. Both are fine. Guessing is not.
- **Evidence:** *"No call notes were transferred. Nothing in the shared drive."*

## farm-07 — Vance Homestead Co

- **Why this needs a person:** Harlan Vance died in February. His two sons are
  farming the operation jointly this season, are in an unresolved estate
  dispute, have each separately described themselves as the person who decides
  on inputs, and there is a lawyer involved. The farm itself is a good target —
  4,600 acres, right crops, and the younger brother is enthusiastic about the
  technology. But there is no identified path to a signature, and Switching
  readiness explicitly scores 0 where there is no identified path to the buyer.
  Outreach here also carries a risk the rubric does not measure: approaching
  either brother can be read as taking a side.
- **The specific question a human has to answer:** Who currently has authority
  to enter an input contract for Vance Homestead, and should we be making
  commercial contact at all before the estate resolves?
- **What we would do once it is answered:** Hold. Re-enter when there is a
  single identified decision maker. The rep has asked for direction and has not
  received it — that is the actionable item here, and it is older than this
  campaign.
- **Evidence:** *"I genuinely do not know who can sign anything right now... Somebody above my level should tell me how to handle this."*

## farm-09 — Willow Slough Partners

- **Why this needs a person:** This is the largest account in the file — 8,900
  acres, ~$1.72M annual input spend, nine years a customer — and it is under an
  explicit customer instruction to make no commercial contact for the remainder
  of the 2026 season. A seed treatment failure in spring 2025 cost them roughly
  1,100 acres of replant and the claim was handled slowly. At the May meeting
  Gwen said they will honour the current program through harvest and will
  initiate contact themselves when ready to discuss 2027.
  **On the numbers alone this farm ranks first in the region.** A ranking sorted
  by value puts it at the top of the outreach list, which is precisely what it
  must not be on. The rep anticipated this in writing.
- **The specific question a human has to answer:** Does the no-contact
  instruction extend to 2027 campaign planning, and who owns the relationship
  repair — the rep, or someone more senior?
- **What we would do once it is answered:** Nothing outbound until the customer
  initiates, per their instruction. Separately and urgently: the claim handling
  is a service failure that no campaign will fix and that nobody currently owns.
- **Evidence:** *"she does not want any commercial contact from us for the remainder of the 2026 season... 'we will call you.'"* And, from the same note:
  *"if someone runs a list by spend they will get this wrong."*
