# Round 1 — do the task yourself, in a chat window

**Time: 8 minutes. Stop when time is called, wherever you have got to.**

Stopping on time matters more than finishing. You are not expected to finish.

> **In class, use the website instead.** The lab site has this brief with a copy
> button, the ten farms one at a time, a countdown, and a completed-farms
> counter. This file is the same material in plain markdown, for anyone working
> offline or reading the repository directly.

---

## Setup

Open any chat LLM you already use — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot,
whichever. Free tier is fine, and whatever you are already signed into is the
right choice. You need nothing else.

Paste the brief below as your first message, then start feeding it rep notes
from `notes-to-paste.md` and working the problem however you would naturally
work it. Ask follow-ups. Push back on its answers. Use it well — this half of
the exercise is not a straw man, and the comparison is worthless if you
sandbag it.

---

## The brief — paste this

> I'm a commercial operations analyst at Bayer Crop Science. I have a regional
> file of 10 US farms. For each one I have a CRM row and a set of field-rep
> call notes written by four different reps over the last eighteen months.
>
> I need to: build a profile of each farm, score all 10 against a rubric, rank
> them, and draft outreach emails to the top five. Anything I can't score
> honestly, I need to set aside for a person to look at rather than guess.
>
> The rubric is: Scale 25%, Portfolio fit 25%, Technology posture 20%,
> Switching readiness 20%, Relationship 10%. Each scored 0–5, total out of 100.
>
> I'll paste the rep notes in. Let's start.

Then paste the notes from `notes-to-paste.md`, and go.

---

## While you work, keep track of two things

1. **How many of the 10 farms you actually completed** — profiled, scored, and
   ready to rank. Not started. Completed.
2. **What you spent the eight minutes doing.** Try to notice whether you were
   thinking about farms or thinking about the chat window — pasting, scrolling,
   re-explaining the rubric, reminding it of something it had already been
   told.

The first poll asks about the first one.

---

## Rules

- **Do not** upload the whole `data/` folder as attachments, and do not ask it
  to go and find the files itself. Work the way most people in most companies
  work: copy, paste, read, respond.
- **Do** use it properly. Ask it to do several farms at once if you want. That
  is a fair thing to try and what happens when you try is part of the point.
- **Do** stop when time is called.
