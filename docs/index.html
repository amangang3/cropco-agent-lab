CropCo Agent Lab

You'll do the same piece of sales work with a GenAI copilot that you already use. Then you hand it to an agentic team and supervise the outputs.

The situation

Which farms do we go after?

You work in commercial operations at CropCo. CropCo sells to about 10,000 of the 40,000 big row-crop farms in the country. Nobody will pay for four times the sales force, so someone has to choose which farms to chase.

What you have

Ten farms. A CRM row for each, plus call notes from four reps over eighteen months.

The job

Profile each farm. Score it against the rubric. Rank them. Write outreach to the top five.

The next thirty minutes

  1. 8 min You do it, in your own chatbot
  2. 4 min Meet the agent team
  3. 5 min Watch them do the same task
  4. 6 min Read what they produced
  5. 7 min Talk about the difference

Work the farms in order and don't skip ahead.

I'm a commercial operations analyst at CropCo. I have a regional file of 10 US farms. For each one I have a CRM row and a set of field-rep call notes written by four different reps over the last eighteen months.

I need to: build a profile of each farm, score all 10 against a rubric, rank them, and draft outreach emails to the top five. Anything I can't score honestly, I need to set aside for a person to look at rather than guess.

The rubric is: Scale 25%, Portfolio fit 25%, Technology posture 20%, Switching readiness 20%, Relationship 10%. Each scored 0–5, total out of 100.

            Reads a farm's CRM row and its rep notes, then writes a standardized profile.

Two copies, five farms each, both at once.

fit-scorer

          
Gives each farm a number

          

            Scores every farm out of 100 against the rubric. But if the
            researcher flagged it, the farm gets no score at all
            and goes on a review list instead, even when it would
            have ranked first without the research flag.

3

          
outreach-writer

          
Drafts the five emails

          

            Gets the ranking and the review list. Writes 120 words to each of

Runs last.
