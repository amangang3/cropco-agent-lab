The situation

Ten farms, four reps, eighteen months of messy notes

You are a commercial operations analyst at Bayer Crop Science. Bayer reaches about 10,000 of the roughly 40,000 major row-crop farms in the United States, and quadrupling the field sales force to reach the rest is not something anyone is going to fund.

What you have

A regional file: ten farms, a CRM row each, and call notes written by four different reps over eighteen months. The notes are uneven, contradictory in places, and occasionally missing. That is on purpose and it is representative.

What the job is

Profile each farm. Score all ten against the rubric. Rank them. Draft outreach to the top five. And set aside anything you cannot judge honestly, for a person to look at, rather than guessing.

The next thirty minutes

  1. 8 min You do it, in your own chatbot
  2. 4 min Meet the agent team
  3. 5 min Watch them do the same task
  4. 6 min Audit what they produced
  5. 7 min Discuss together

You are not expected to finish part one. Where you get to in eight minutes is the measurement — it is the number we compare everything else against. Do not skip ahead to part three; the order is the point.

Part 2 · 4 minutes · together

        
Meet the team you are about to hand it to

        

          Three agents. Nobody wrote code to build them — each one is a page of
          plain English saying who it is, what it is allowed to touch, and when
          it must stop. That page is the whole design.
        

      


      

        

          
1

          
farm-researcher

          
Reads · does not judge

          

            Takes a CRM row and a rep's call notes and turns them into a
            structured profile. It is told to flag problems rather than
            tidy them away: contradictions, missing information, unclear
            authority, and any instruction not to contact the customer.
          

          
Two copies run at once, five farms each.

        


        

          
2

          
fit-scorer

          
Scores · and refuses to

          

            Applies the rubric — and only the rubric. It also carries the rule
            that decides this exercise: a farm carrying a flag is not
            scored at all. Not scored low. Absent, with a written reason,
            even if it would obviously rank first.
          

          
Cannot start until all ten profiles exist.

        


        

          
3

          
outreach-writer

          
Writes · to five people

          

            Drafts the outreach. It is given the ranked list and the
            human-review list, so it knows who not to write to
            rather than only who to write to. 120 words, one specific detail, one
            small ask.
          

          
Runs last, on the top five only.

  • No agent is the boss. There is no manager agent. The order is fixed by the person who set the team up: research, then score, then write.
    • 
        
  • They never talk to each other. Every handoff is a file. One agent writes it, the next one reads it. That is the entire communication protocol, and it is why you can audit what happened.
    • 
        
  • The judgement is in the writing, not the model. When you see it stop on a farm in a moment, the reason will be a sentence somebody typed — not something the model worked out.
